What this value is

The inviscid pressure (wave) drag of the nose and the body ahead of the base, on the body's cross-section, at zero incidence: not the rocket's total drag, which also has skin friction, base drag and fins.

The value shown is the finest grid's; its band is measured from the grids and includes an allowance of 7e-5 of the value for an error the grids cannot show. That band is the numerical error of the inviscid solve; the model's own error is not in it: wind-tunnel pressures on two ogives at Mach 1.98 differ from the inviscid value by 3% and 8 to 9% (the comparison).
