Nose wave drag

The pressure drag of a rocket's nose and body ahead of the base, in supersonic flow at zero incidence, with its numerical error measured: each case is solved on successively finer grids, and the error band is measured from how the drag changes between them. Needs a browser with WebGPU. Back to bowshock

Cells Iterations Cd Time

What this is: the inviscid pressure (wave) drag coefficient of the nose and the body ahead of the base, on the body's cross-sectional area, for sharp noses with an attached shock. It excludes skin friction, base drag and fins, so it is not a rocket's total drag. Validated against exact solutions on cones (Taylor-Maccoll) and on a tangent ogive (method of characteristics); the error band also contains a measured allowance for an error the grid family cannot show.
