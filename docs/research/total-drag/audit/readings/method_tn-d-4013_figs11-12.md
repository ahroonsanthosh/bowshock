# TN D-4013 figs. 11 and 12, CA,corr and CA,b against M: second reading, method

Source: NTRS 19670020050.pdf, PDF pages 62 (fig 11, short model) and 63 (fig 12, long model), rendered at 300 dpi (native 1-bit scans). Done before opening the first agent's readings.

Calibration: vertical lines every 0.05 in M found by column profiles in two bands (they do not lean, <1 px); labels .5 to 1.3 checked. Fig 11: 1472 px per unit M, fig 12: 1410 px per unit M. CA,corr: horizontal lines every 0.05 (fig 11: 144.6 px per 0.1 at the left edge only; fig 12: 135.1 px per 0.1 across the full width). CA,b: 144.6 (fig 11) and 135 (fig 12) px per 0.1.

Fig 11 is sheared: the CA,corr = 0 line falls from y 2086.0 at x 650 to 2076 at x 1745, and the CA,b = 0 line by the same 10 px, while the vertical lines stay vertical; all horizontal lines measured at the left edge show the same slope (0.6 to 1.4 px per 100 px). Values were therefore referred to the local zero line at each M (2078.4 at M 1.0, 2077.4 at 1.1, 2076.3 at 1.2). Reading the left-edge lines as level instead raises fig 11 CA,corr by 0.005 to 0.007; both are listed. Fig 12 lines are level within 1.5 px.

Points: column scans (scripts/curve.py) a few px either side of each M line, interpolated to M = 1.0, 1.1, 1.2 (the curves end at the M = 1.2 line, so 1.2 is a 1 to 2 px extrapolation). Line styles identified on zoom views (dashed above solid throughout M 0.95 to 1.2 on both figures; dash-dot = fins off). The fins-off curve is nearly vertical at M = 1.0, so +-2 px in x moves it by +-0.002.

Estimated error: +-0.002 (fig 12) to +-0.003 (fig 11, plus the shear choice) in CA,corr; +-0.002 in CA,b.
