# Basic Finner geometry: second transcription (DREV TM 9703, fig. 2 PDF p. 53; text pp. 3 to 4 (PDF pp. 16 to 17); tables I and II PDF pp. 36 to 37)

All in calibres, 1 cal = 30.0 mm nominal (table I d = 30.00 mm; table II measured 29.746 to 29.979 mm, length 299.364 to 300.127 mm).

- Overall length 10.0 cal (table I l = 30.00 cm).
- Nose: cone, 10 deg half angle (fig. 2 "10 deg"; text "20 deg nose cone"), length 2.84 cal (0.5/tan 10 deg = 2.836), tip radius 0.004 cal ("meplat").
- Body: cylinder 1.0 cal diameter from 2.84 cal to the base at 10.0 cal; flat base.
- Fins: four, rectangular, 1.0 cal chord by 1.0 cal span (exposed, measured from the body surface), trailing edges flush with the base; section a single wedge ("conical in shape") from a sharp leading edge (radius 0.004 cal) to 0.08 cal thickness at the base of the fin, i.e. a blunt trailing edge 0.08 cal thick at the body base (fig. 2 side view shows the wedge growing aft to the 0.08 dimension at the base).
- Fin cant 0, 2, 4 deg about an axis 0.50 cal ahead of the base (fig. 2 lower view).
- CG 5.50 cal from the nose (table I 16.50 cm; table II 5.485 to 5.541 cal).
- Mass 1.58 kg; Ix 1.92 kg cm2; Iy 97.85 kg cm2 (table I).
- Reference area: cross-sectional area of the projectile (nomenclature, PDF p. 12).
- Materials: steel fins and cylinder, high-density alloy nose (text p. 4).
- Range: 20 C, standard atmosphere, 45% relative humidity (text p. 3); Re on length 6.99e6 to 29.9e6 (table V).
