Pairing of the two blind readings of the measured nose pressures (0014 10.7 (w))

Paired 2026-10-24 by a neutral party that read only the two readers' folders
and the readers' brief (../reader-brief.md). The pairing is reader A against
reader B only. No earlier typing in the repository was opened or compared, and
nothing was compared with a computed solution. No winner was picked and no
value was averaged: every reading stays as each reader gave it, in A/ and B/.

Files
  A/, B/        each reader's method.md, geometry.csv and six reading CSVs,
                copied unchanged. The PDFs, rendered images and the readers'
                scripts and text layers are not committed.
  hashes.txt    the four source PDFs (same for both readers).
  pairing/      one pairing CSV per reading CSV, geometry_pairing.csv, and
                pair.py (the script that wrote the reading pairings;
                run: python3 -I pair.py <folder holding A and B> <out folder>).

Rules used
  Tables (TN 3716 table I(a), RM A53E01 table II(a), TN 4233 tables I(a) and
  I(b)): station and Cp agree only on an exact match of the printed number.
  A leading zero added or left off (.072 against 0.072) is the same printed
  number and counts as a match; reader A wrote leading zeros, reader B kept
  the printed form.
  Figures (TN 3716 fig. 3, RM E51H17 fig. 4(a)): station and Cp agree when
  the difference is no larger than A's stated read error plus B's stated read
  error for that point.
    TN 3716 fig. 3: A +-0.02 x/d, +-0.001 Cp; B +-0.02 x/d, +-0.003 Cp;
      summed +-0.04 x/d, +-0.004 Cp.
    RM E51H17 fig. 4(a): A +-0.05 in., +-0.0003 Cp for clear symbols and
      +-0.08 in., +-0.0006 Cp for partly hidden ones; B +-0.07 in., +-0.0005
      Cp for clear symbols and +-0.001 Cp for overlapping ones. Summed per
      point (0.0008 to 0.0016 Cp, 0.12 to 0.15 in.).
  Matching: tables by station (TN 4233 by Mach row and orifice number);
  TN 3716 fig. 3 by order along x; RM E51H17 fig. 4(a) by symbol (circle =
  meridian 0 deg, square = meridian 90 deg) then by order along x.
  Flags and alternatives are copied into the pairing CSVs as each reader gave
  them. A cell flagged by one or both readers is still "agree" when the two
  main readings match; the flags are listed below and stay open.

Source PDFs

Readers' notes carried forward (unresolved; for the coordinator)

     Reynolds number of approximately 0.5x10^6 based on body diameter; the
     Tests text (PDF p5) and the table II heading (PDF p22) say 0.5x10^6 per
     inch. Recorded by B; A recorded per inch only, from the text and the
     table heading. Not resolved here.

     PDF p1 to 14, B read p3 to 13 through the text layer and checked key
     pages on the image; B notes a statement the OCR missed cannot be ruled
     out). This matters for the u_meas term: there is no stated measurement
     uncertainty to use for this set.

     radial-angle columns (0 to 270 deg). Both readers found that model 2 has
     one orifice row at theta = 0 and was rolled for the other angles, so the
     columns are roll positions; under the brief's zero-roll rule both read
     only the theta = 0 column. B notes the other columns are clear and easy
     to read if they are wanted.
