Normalisation of each set (0014 10.7 (af), addendum (b))
=========================================================

Written by the pairing party on 2026-10-27, before any value of either
reading was compared with anything. Taken only from what the two readers
recorded (geometry.csv "pressure normalisation" rows, the value_quantity
column of their CSVs, and their method notes), with their quotes of the
reports. The pairing party did not read the reports to decide this.

Addendum (b): a set tests P1 (p at most p02 on the face) only where its
face pressures are given over a reference not measured on the face. A set
whose pressures are given over its own measured stagnation (pitot)
pressure does not test P1. Where the readers differ or the report as
quoted is unclear, the answer here is "not established".


1. NACA TN 4300 (figs. 7 and 16; 4-in. model D, 12-in. model A, and the
   two comparison series of fig. 7)

   Reader A, geometry.csv: "p_l/p_o" ... "text PDF p. 7: pressures
   presented 'as fractions of the total pressure behind the shock'; axis
   label p_l/p_o; subscript o = stagnation point".
   Reader A, fig. 7 value_quantity: "p_l/p_o as the axis states it (p_o:
   stagnation-point pressure, which the text calls the total pressure
   behind the shock)".
   Reader A, accuracy row: "the solid symbols were measured by low-range
   cells (difference from the centre-tube pressure) and are considerably
   more reliable than the open symbols, measured with total-pressure
   instruments of higher range".

   Reader B, geometry.csv: "p_l/p_o" ... "axes of figs. 7 and 16 read
   p_l/p_o; subscript o = stagnation point (symbols list); text: pressures
   presented as fractions of the total pressure behind the shock".
   Reader B, fig. 7 value_quantity: "p_l/p_o (right-hand axis; local
   static pressure over stagnation-point pressure; text calls it a
   fraction of the total pressure behind the shock)".
   Reader B, accuracy row: "solid symbols of fig. 7 were measured by
   low-range cells (difference from the centre tube) and are considerably
   more reliable than the open symbols (higher-range total-pressure
   instruments)".

   Finding: NOT ESTABLISHED. Both readers record the same quantity
   (p_l/p_o, o the stagnation point, "the total pressure behind the
   shock"), but neither records whether p_o is the pressure measured at
   the face centre or a value from the stated Mach number and jet total
   pressure. Both record that a centre pressure was measured (the
   "centre-tube" pressure the low-range cells were referred to), which
   does not say that the plotted ratio divides by it. Whether TN 4300
   tests P1 is therefore not established from the readings. The same
   holds for fig. 7's comparison series (4-in. model, Unitary tunnel,
   M = 2.49; 3-in. model, ref. 3, M = 2.01), plotted on the same p_l/p_o
   axis; neither reader records their reference.

2. NACA RM L57C18 (fig. 5, models B, C, D, F)

   Reader A, geometry.csv: "C_p = (p_l - p_inf)/q_inf" ... "symbols list:
   pressure coefficient (p_l - p_inf)/q_inf; fig. 5 axis C_p".
   Reader B, geometry.csv: "C_p = (p_l - p_inf)/q_inf" ... "symbols list;
   figure 5 axis C_p".
   value_quantity, A: "C_p = (p_l - p_inf)/q_inf as the axis states it";
   B: "C_p, pressure coefficient (p_l - p_inf)/q_inf, as the axis
   states".

   Finding: the readers agree. The pressures are given over the free
   stream (p_inf and q_inf), a reference not measured on the face. RM
   L57C18 does NOT normalise by its own measured stagnation pressure, so
   it TESTS P1.

3. NASA TM X-19 (fig. 5, M = 2.49)

   Reader A, geometry.csv: "p_l/p_t,2" ... "text PDF p. 5: local pressures
   on the front face reduced to p_l/p_t,2, p_t,2 measured by the orifice
   at the centre of the flat face at alpha 0; fig. 5 caption says the
   same in words".
   Reader B, geometry.csv: "p_l/p_t,2" ... "text: p_t,2 measured by an
   orifice at the centre of the flat face at alpha 0; caption: ratio to
   the local pressure at the stagnation point for alpha 0".
   value_quantity, A: "(text: p_t,2 measured by the orifice at the face
   centre at alpha 0)"; B: "text: p_t,2 measured by the centre orifice at
   alpha 0".

   Finding: the readers agree. TM X-19 normalises by its own measured
   stagnation-point pressure (the face-centre orifice at alpha 0), so it
   DOES NOT TEST P1 (P1 holds there by construction).

4. NASA TN D-5392 (tables I, II, III, model 1; M = 2.49, 4.06, 4.63)

   Reader A, geometry.csv: "p_l/p_t,2" ... "table heading; p_t,2 =
   stagnation conditions behind a normal shock at the free-stream Mach
   number (symbols); text PDF p. 8 implies p_t,2 is computed".
   Reader A, value_quantity: "p_l/p_t,2 (table heading; p_t,2: stagnation
   pressure behind a normal shock at the free-stream Mach number)".
   Reader B, geometry.csv: "p_l/p_t,2" ... "table heading; p_t,2 =
   stagnation conditions behind a normal shock at the free-stream Mach
   number (symbols list)".
   Reader B, accuracy row: "mercury manometers within 0.5 psf;
   stagnation-point pressure by precision mercury manometer, 1-psi
   differential gage readings subtracted from it".
   Reader B, value_quantity: "p_l/p_t,2 (local surface pressure over
   stagnation pressure behind a normal shock at the free-stream Mach
   number), as the table heads it".

   Finding: NOT ESTABLISHED. Both readers quote the same heading and the
   same symbols-list definition. Reader A adds that the text "implies"
   p_t,2 is computed, an inference, not a quote. Reader B draws no
   conclusion and records that the stagnation-point pressure was measured
   (precision mercury manometer), without saying whether the tables
   divide by that measured value or by one from the free-stream Mach
   number. The report as quoted is unclear on this point, so whether TN
   D-5392 tests P1 is not established from the readings.


Summary
-------

   Set            Reference (as both readers record it)        Own measured p_t2?   Tests P1?
   TN 4300        p_l/p_o, o = stagnation point                not established      not established
   RM L57C18      C_p = (p_l - p_inf)/q_inf (free stream)      no                   yes
   TM X-19        p_l/p_t,2, p_t,2 measured at face centre     yes                  no
   TN D-5392      p_l/p_t,2, p_t,2 behind normal shock at M    not established      not established

Of the four sets, only RM L57C18 is established as testing P1; TM X-19 is
established as not testing it; for TN 4300 and TN D-5392 it is not
established. RM L57C18's face orifices lie on faces with corner radii of
1.0, 0.4 and 0.1 in. (and model F's recessed face), on cone frusta, not
cylinders (both readers); see the location pairs for which of its points
each reader puts on the face.
