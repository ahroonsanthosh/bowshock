Trial solves of the flat face at M 2.0, coarsest grid (36 x 24), before the registered runs
(design record 0016 section 21.3). The script appended to out/ and the first runs' files were
deleted before later runs, so their lines are not here; their values, from the session's log:

1. HLLC, block implicit, CFL to 20, radius 0.04, 30000 steps: not settled (residual 6.6e-3 of
   the start, the face mean changing by 0.073 between checks 100 steps apart); at the end the
   straight face's largest wall Mach number 0.69, least wall pressure 0.727 of p02, the sonic
   point on the rounding, the face mean (gauge over the pitot's) 0.894.
2. HLLC, block implicit, CFL to 5, radius 0.04, 8000 steps: the face mean at each 1000 steps
   0.901, 0.806, 0.768, 0.942, 0.924, 0.915, 0.883, 0.788; the largest residual at the face's
   cells next to the rounding.
3. HLLC, explicit local steps, radius 0.04, 20000 steps: the face mean at each 1000 steps
   between 0.796 and 0.934; the largest residual at the rounding and just behind it.
4. HLL, block implicit, CFL to 5, radius 0.04: a non-physical state after 724 steps, in a cell
   on the plate's side near the outflow.
