Supersonic rocket aerodynamics

Shock waves around your rocket, solved on your own GPU.

bowshock is an open-source flow solver that runs in your browser. It is in early development: today it computes the inviscid forebody wave drag of sharp and ellipsoid noses, not a rocket's total drag, with its measured error band.
