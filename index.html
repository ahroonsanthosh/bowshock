bowshock

Supersonic rocket aerodynamics in your browser, on your own GPU. Early development: the inviscid forebody wave drag of sharp and ellipsoid noses, with its measured error band, and from it a finned rocket's total drag, validated for one class of rocket only.

Demos

  • Nose wave drag and total drag: the inviscid pressure (wave) drag of a sharp or ellipsoid nose and the body behind it, with its error band or marked not verified; and the rocket's total drag with fins and base, labelled validated on the Basic Finner inside that rocket's class, an estimate, not validated, outside it, or refused with the reasons.
  • Supersonic ramp: oblique shocks and expansion fans, live, against exact theory.
  • Shock tube: the 1D reference solver against exact solutions.
